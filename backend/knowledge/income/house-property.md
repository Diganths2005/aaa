# House-property income

The engine supports self-occupied, let-out, and deemed-let-out profile scenarios. For let-out or deemed-let-out property it applies the documented 30% statutory deduction. Self-occupied loan-interest limits are applied only after the required loan facts are validated. Old-regime inter-head loss set-off is limited to the implemented Rs 2 lakh rule; the new regime does not set off a current house-property loss against other heads.
