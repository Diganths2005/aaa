# Old tax regime

The old-regime slab schedule is maintained in the AY 2026-27 tax-engine rules. Individuals have a nil slab up to Rs 2.5 lakh; resident senior and super-senior categories use their configured higher nil-slab thresholds. The engine applies supported deductions, rebate, surcharge, cess, tax-payment reconciliation, and rounding. Consult the engine result for a taxpayer-specific comparison; this note is not a calculation.
