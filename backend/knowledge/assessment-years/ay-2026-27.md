# Assessment year 2026-27

TaxWise currently supports assessment year 2026-27, corresponding to financial year 2025-26. The active calculation rules are maintained in `backend/tax_engine/rules/ay_2026_27/` and are the authority for all numerical results.

The preparation workflow supports ITR-1 within its documented eligibility limits and a constrained ITR-3 business-income review summary for supported resident, non-presumptive profiles with non-negative user-entered net profit. Full statutory ITR-3 schedules and ITR-2/ITR-4 preparation are outside the implemented scope. The system does not file or submit returns.
