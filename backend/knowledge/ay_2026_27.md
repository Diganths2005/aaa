# TaxWise AY 2026-27 knowledge notes

source: Income Tax Department AY 2026-27 individual return guidance and TaxWise rules package

ITR-1 is limited to the supported resident salary or pension scenarios without business income, unsupported capital gains, foreign schedules, speculative income, or unlisted equity.

ITR-2 is the supported return family for resident or non-resident individuals with supported salary, house property, other sources, capital-gain transactions, or foreign-information scenarios. Foreign schedules remain partial in TaxWise.

ITR-3 is selected for supported business or professional income that is not limited to the supported presumptive scenarios. Detailed business expenses, balance-sheet schedules, and loss schedules remain unsupported.

ITR-4 is selected only for supported presumptive business or professional income with no capital gains, foreign schedules, speculative income, or unlisted equity.

Tax amounts, regime comparisons, deductions, capital gains, and eligibility results must come from the deterministic TaxWise engine. AI explains those results and does not calculate or change confirmed profile data.
