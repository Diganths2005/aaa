# Return selection

Return selection evaluates the taxpayer profile and reports a likely return family with reasons, missing information, and unsupported conditions. The selector can identify ITR-1, ITR-2, ITR-3, or ITR-4 scenarios. Preparation summaries are available for ITR-1 and a constrained ITR-3 business-income subset; ITR-2/ITR-4 and complete statutory ITR-3 schedules are not prepared. A recommendation does not by itself imply preparation support; unsupported profiles must receive the selector's explanation and must not be converted into a different form.
