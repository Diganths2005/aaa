# ITR-1 preparation

ITR-1 preparation is limited to the supported AY 2026-27 resident scenarios with salary or pension income and supported other-source, house-property, deduction, and tax-payment details. Business or professional income, capital gains, foreign schedules, speculative income, unlisted equity, and non-resident cases are outside this ITR-1 preparation scope. The eligibility service decides whether preparation can proceed. Preparation and PDF output are review summaries, not official filing or submission.
