# Tax deducted at source

TDS amounts are taken from confirmed salary, pension, other-income, or tax-payment profile entries and included in the deterministic total-tax-paid figure. The engine reconciles total tax paid with final liability to produce refund or balance payable. It does not independently verify a tax-credit statement.
